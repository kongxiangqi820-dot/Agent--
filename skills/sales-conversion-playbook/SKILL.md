# Sales Conversion Playbook

## Goal
Turn vague sales intent into a staged conversion plan.

## Workflow
1. Classify customer stage: cold, warm, trial, negotiation, closing.
2. Capture customer profile: budget, decision maker, urgency, key objections.
3. Build action plan:
   - first message or call opener
   - objection response
   - next-step commitment
   - follow-up rhythm
4. Define success metric for this stage.

## Output
1. Stage diagnosis
2. Talk track (short version)
3. Follow-up cadence (D1/D3/D7)
4. Risks and fallback

## Constraints
- Avoid exaggerated claims.
- Keep benefits tied to evidence and scenario.
- Always include next-step CTA.
